Privacy policy

iOS app · Updated 4 October 2026

Bible of Berea is provided by GNABORET. Contact gnaboret@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Your Scripture library

The app stores your reading position, preferences, favorites, notes, markings, study history, and memory progress locally on your device. The app does not send this library to GNABORET or its advertising provider. No app account is required.

If you export, copy, or share text or a backup, the destination you choose receives that information. Handle backups as private files. Deleting the app removes its local data; separately saved exports remain where you put them.

Advertisements

The app uses Google AdMob for banner advertisements in Settings. The advertising SDK may collect an IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad interaction data, performance information, and crash diagnostics. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. Collection depends on device permissions and applicable consent choices.

The app uses Google's consent system where required. Available ad privacy choices can be reopened from Settings → Remove ads. Removing ads stops banner requests after Apple confirms ownership. The app does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone for advertising.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure for the provider's practices and retention information.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time Remove Ads purchase. The app checks Apple-verified purchase information to determine whether ads should be removed. GNABORET does not receive your payment card details. See Apple's privacy information.

Speech and links

Read aloud uses installed Apple voices. External study links open through your browser; the destination website's privacy policy applies.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your request. You may ask us to delete your support correspondence, subject to any records we must retain.

This website

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not include our own analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking cookies. GitHub processes connection information to operate the hosting service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this page when these practices change. The date above identifies the current policy.
