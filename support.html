How can we help?

Email gnaboret@gmail.com for support, feedback, or privacy questions. Include your device model, app version, and a short description of what happened. Please avoid sending private notes or a backup unless needed.

Read and compare

Tap the translation name beside the center passage control to choose a Bible. Open Settings → Bible & layout to switch between one Bible and two mirrors.

Explore a verse

Tap its verse number to open Explore. Commentary and referenced passages are available there. Word information depends on the selected translation and its available study data.

Keep your library

Your reading position, favorites, notes, markings, and memory progress are saved on your device. Use Settings → Data to export a backup before moving devices or deleting the app. Keep exported backups somewhere you trust.

Remove Ads

The iOS release offers a one-time purchase under Settings → Remove ads. To recover a purchase, sign in with the same Apple Account and choose Restore purchases on that page. Apple handles payment and shows your local price before confirmation. There is no subscription.

For purchase or refund help, use Apple's purchase support. Apple and Google purchases belong to their respective stores.

Read aloud

The iOS app uses installed Apple speech voices. Voice availability varies by language and device.
